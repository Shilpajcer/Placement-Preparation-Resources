# 📚 Aptitude – Basic Formulas

A quick formula sheet for placement aptitude preparation.

---

## 1️⃣ Number System

### Basic Formulas
- Even number → divisible by 2
- Odd number → not divisible by 2
- Prime number → exactly 2 factors: 1 and itself
- Composite number → more than 2 factors

### Divisibility Rules
- 2 → last digit is even
- 3 → sum of digits is divisible by 3
- 4 → last 2 digits are divisible by 4
- 5 → last digit is 0 or 5
- 6 → divisible by both 2 and 3
- 8 → last 3 digits are divisible by 8
- 9 → sum of digits is divisible by 9
- 10 → last digit is 0
- 11 → difference between sums of alternate digits is 0 or divisible by 11

---

## 2️⃣ HCF & LCM

- HCF → Highest Common Factor
- LCM → Least Common Multiple

For two positive numbers:

**HCF × LCM = Product of the two numbers**

Example:

12 × 18 = 216

HCF = 6, LCM = 36

6 × 36 = 216

## 3️⃣ Percentage

### Basic Formula

**Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100**

### Percentage Change

**Percentage Change = (Change / Original Value) × 100**

### Increase

**New Value = Original × (100 + Increase%) / 100**

### Decrease

**New Value = Original × (100 - Decrease%) / 100**

### Successive Percentage Change

For changes of `a%` and `b%`:

**Net Change = a + b + (ab / 100)**

Use negative values for decreases.

---

## 4️⃣ Profit, Loss & Discount

### Profit

**Profit = SP - CP**

### Loss

**Loss = CP - SP**

### Profit Percentage

**Profit% = (Profit / CP) × 100**

### Loss Percentage

**Loss% = (Loss / CP) × 100**

### Selling Price

**SP = CP × (100 + Profit%) / 100**

**SP = CP × (100 - Loss%) / 100**

### Discount

**Discount = MP - SP**

**Discount% = (Discount / MP) × 100**

Where:

- CP = Cost Price
- SP = Selling Price
- MP = Marked Price

---

## 5️⃣ Ratio & Proportion

### Ratio

**a : b = a / b**

### Proportion

If:

**a : b = c : d**

Then:

**a × d = b × c**

### Division in a Ratio

If total amount is divided in ratio `a : b`:

First share:

**Total × a / (a + b)**

Second share:

**Total × b / (a + b)**

---

## 6️⃣ Average

### Basic Formula

**Average = Sum of Values / Number of Values**

Therefore:

**Sum = Average × Number of Values**

### Combined Average

**Combined Average = (n₁a₁ + n₂a₂) / (n₁ + n₂)**

Where:

- n = number of values
- a = average

---

## 7️⃣ Time, Speed & Distance

### Basic Formulas

**Speed = Distance / Time**

**Distance = Speed × Time**

**Time = Distance / Speed**

### Unit Conversion

**km/hr → m/sec**

Multiply by **5/18**

**m/sec → km/hr**

Multiply by **18/5**

### Relative Speed

Same direction:

**Relative Speed = Difference of Speeds**

Opposite direction:

**Relative Speed = Sum of Speeds**

---

## 8️⃣ Time & Work

### Basic Formula

**Work = Rate × Time**

If A completes work in `x` days:

**A's 1-day work = 1/x**

If A and B work together:

**Combined Work = 1/x + 1/y**

### Work Formula

**Work = Number of People × Days × Efficiency**

For the same work:

**People × Days = Constant**

---

## 9️⃣ Simple Interest

### Formula

**SI = (P × R × T) / 100**

### Amount

**Amount = P + SI**

Where:

- P = Principal
- R = Rate
- T = Time

---

## 🔟 Compound Interest

### Amount

**A = P(1 + R/100)ᵀ**

### Compound Interest

**CI = A - P**

Where:

- P = Principal
- R = Rate
- T = Time
- A = Amount

---

## 1️⃣1️⃣ Ages

### Basic Rules

After `n` years:

**Present Age + n**

Before `n` years:

**Present Age - n**

### Age Difference

The age difference between two people remains constant.

---

## 1️⃣2️⃣ Pipes & Cisterns

If a pipe fills a tank in `x` hours:

**1-hour work = 1/x**

If a pipe empties a tank in `y` hours:

**1-hour work = -1/y**

Together:

**Net Work = Filling Rate - Emptying Rate**

---

## 1️⃣3️⃣ Partnership

Profit is divided according to:

**Capital × Time**

If:

A invests `P₁` for `T₁`

B invests `P₂` for `T₂`

Then:

**A's Share : B's Share = P₁T₁ : P₂T₂**

---

## 1️⃣4️⃣ Probability

### Basic Formula

**Probability = Favorable Outcomes / Total Outcomes**

### Range

**0 ≤ Probability ≤ 1**

### Complement

**P(Not A) = 1 - P(A)**

---

## 1️⃣5️⃣ Permutation & Combination

### Factorial

**n! = n × (n-1) × (n-2) × ... × 1**

Also:

**0! = 1**

### Permutation

Arrangement matters.

**nPr = n! / (n-r)!**

### Combination

Selection matters.

**nCr = n! / [r!(n-r)!]**

---

## 1️⃣6️⃣ Mixture & Alligation

For two quantities:

**Cheaper : Dearer = (Dearer - Mean) : (Mean - Cheaper)**

Used when mixing two items to obtain a desired average price/value.

---

## 1️⃣7️⃣ Mensuration

### Rectangle

**Area = Length × Breadth**

**Perimeter = 2(Length + Breadth)**

### Square

**Area = Side²**

**Perimeter = 4 × Side**

### Triangle

**Area = ½ × Base × Height**

### Circle

**Area = πr²**

**Circumference = 2πr**

### Cuboid

**Volume = Length × Breadth × Height**

**Total Surface Area = 2(lb + bh + lh)**

### Cylinder

**Volume = πr²h**

### Percentage Decrease

**Decrease% = (Decrease / Original) × 100**
