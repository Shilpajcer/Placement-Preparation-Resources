# 🧮 Aptitude Questions with Solutions

A collection of placement-style aptitude questions with short and easy-to-understand solutions.

## 1️⃣ Percentages

### Question 1
A number is increased by 20%. What is the new value if the original number is 500?

**Solution:**

Increase = 20% of 500

= (20/100) × 500

= 100

New value = 500 + 100

**Answer: 600**

---

### Question 2
A number is decreased by 25%. If the original number is 800, find the new value.

**Solution:**

Decrease = (25/100) × 800

= 200

New value = 800 - 200

**Answer: 600**

---

## 2️⃣ Profit & Loss

### Question 3
A product is bought for ₹500 and sold for ₹600. Find the profit percentage.

**Solution:**

Profit = SP - CP

= 600 - 500

= ₹100

Profit% = (Profit / CP) × 100

= (100/500) × 100

**Answer: 20%**

---

### Question 4
A product is bought for ₹800 and sold for ₹720. Find the loss percentage.

**Solution:**

Loss = CP - SP

= 800 - 720

= ₹80

Loss% = (80/800) × 100

**Answer: 10%**

## 3️⃣ Ratio & Proportion

### Question 5
The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 3:2. If there are 30 boys, how many girls are there?

**Solution:**

3 parts = 30

1 part = 30/3 = 10

Girls = 2 × 10

**Answer: 20 girls**

---

## 4️⃣ Average

### Question 6
Find the average of 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50.

**Solution:**

Sum = 10 + 20 + 30 + 40 + 50

= 150

Number of values = 5

Average = 150/5

**Answer: 30**

## 5️⃣ Time, Speed & Distance

### Question 7
A car travels 120 km in 3 hours. Find its speed.

**Solution:**

Speed = Distance / Time

= 120/3

**Answer: 40 km/hr**

---

### Question 8
A train travels at 60 km/hr for 2 hours. Find the distance travelled.

**Solution:**

Distance = Speed × Time

= 60 × 2

**Answer: 120 km**

---

## 6️⃣ Time & Work

### Question 9
A person can complete a work in 10 days. What part of the work is completed in one day?

**Solution:**

One-day work = 1 / Total Days

= 1/10

**Answer: 1/10 of the work**

---

### Question 10
A can complete a work in 10 days and B can complete it in 15 days. How many days will they take together?

**Solution:**

A's one-day work = 1/10

B's one-day work = 1/15

Together:

1/10 + 1/15

LCM = 30

= 3/30 + 2/30

= 5/30

= 1/6

Therefore, they complete the work in:

**Answer: 6 days**

## 7️⃣ Simple Interest

### Question 11
Find the simple interest on ₹5000 at 10% per annum for 2 years.

**Solution:**

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

= (5000 × 10 × 2) / 100

**Answer: ₹1000**

---

## 8️⃣ Probability

### Question 12
A coin is tossed once. What is the probability of getting a Head?

**Solution:**

Favourable outcomes = 1

Total outcomes = 2

Probability = 1/2

**Answer: 1/2**

---

## 9️⃣ Ages

### Question 13
A person's present age is 20 years. What will be their age after 5 years?

**Solution:**

Future age = Present age + Number of years

= 20 + 5

**Answer: 25 years**

## 🔟 Number System

### Question 14
Which of the following numbers is divisible by 3?

**123, 124, 125, 127**

**Solution:**

For divisibility by 3, the sum of digits must be divisible by 3.

For 123:

1 + 2 + 3 = 6

6 is divisible by 3.

**Answer: 123**
